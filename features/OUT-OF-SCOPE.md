# Out of scope — Handled

The fence for Handled, scoped 2026-10-17 late evening (docket dates are UTC, 2026-10-18) in a deliberately short interview: three decisions and one readback, with the checklist sweep and the adversarial pass declined to start building. Every entry cites the docket ruling it came from and the date that ruling was made. Bound scenarios touched: none (a new project; nothing is bound yet).

## Declined

- **The textbook chore.** Identifying a textbook and finding it cheaper or as a rental. Offered as the fourth chore, not selected. (D22, 2026-10-18)
- **The checklist sweep and the adversarial pass.** Usability, reliability, availability, performance, security, ops burden, diagnosability, evolvability, lifecycle and compliance were declined in bulk, unprobed; no objection to the corpus was put or answered. The first judge's question may be the first objection it meets. (D23, 2026-10-18)
- **A mobile app.** The laptop web app with its webcam is the only surface; the phone receives texts only. (D24, 2026-10-18)

## Deferred — each with its reopening condition

- **Tickets from cities other than Los Angeles.** Best effort: the agent searches for that city's pay-or-contest page and proceeds if it finds one, else stops as a failed step. The demo uses a Los Angeles ticket. Reopens if a judge brings a ticket from another city. (D27, 2026-10-18)

Beyond this, what may go unbuilt is governed by the cut order in `DESIGN.md` (D29, ratified D34): the bill chore first, then the Buy step in favour of the ranked card alone, then the day-before text; identification, the ticket chore, the Confirm gate and the report are never cut. Anything unbuilt at the deadline is to be recorded here, under this heading, by the build.

## Named assumptions

- **A test card at the demo.** The card on file is a test card, so a real site declines it or a sandbox accepts it and no real money moves; the demo script stops at Confirm and does not tap it. Reopens if a judge wants to see a real payment go through. (D25, 2026-10-18)
- **No logins.** The user has no saved logins and Handled holds no passwords; a page that demands a login is a failed step. Reopens if the visionary wants saved logins for a known biller or store. (D26, 2026-10-18)
- **Fields are shown before they are used.** A misread digit in a citation number or a wrong amount on a bill reaches a form only if the user lets it: the card shows every field it read before the browser acts, the user corrects a field by saying or typing it, and the Confirm gate is the last guard. Reopens if a judge's ticket is misread at the demo. (D28, 2026-10-18; ratified D33)

## Out of reach by construction — stated so nobody expects it

- **Anything behind a login.** Account pages, saved payment methods, order history. Every chore runs as a stranger to the site. (D26, 2026-10-18)
- **Whether the chore was right to do.** Handled pays what the bill says and contests what the user says; it does not check that the ticket was fair or the bill correct. The Confirm pause is where the user does that. (D7, 2026-10-18)

## Roads not taken

- Chores: the textbook chore. Three of four offered chores were selected. (D1, 2026-10-18)
- Autonomy: fully autonomous (pay, then report); a demo mode wired to a sandbox card with no pause. The visionary chose stop-and-ask every time. (D2, 2026-10-18)
- Hand-off surface: leaving the visible browser on the final page as the result. The card plus texts were chosen; the browser need not stay open. (D3, 2026-10-18)
